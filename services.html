Frequently Answered Inquiries

Common questions regarding our onboarding scripts, ad integrations, and campaign telemetry.

How long does campaign onboarding take?

Typical setup takes 7 to 10 business days. During this timeframe, we audit your historical GA4 telemetry events, resolve tag integrations, and link visual dashboard pipelines.

Do you lock client profiles into long-term contracts?

No, all programs operate on a month-to-month subscription model. You can pause, upgrade, or scale down scaling tiers freely with 14 days' notice.

What reporting frequencies do you maintain?

You will receive real-time dashboard panel coordinates populated with Google Search Console, paid performance data, and event conversion charts. Bi-weekly review synchronizations verify scaling milestones.

Will I own the ad profiles and account structures?

Yes. Unlike other agencies that retain account access, you maintain full ownership of all custom structures, ad copies, tracking configurations, and Looker dashboards we deploy.
